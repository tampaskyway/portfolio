About me

Hello! My name is Vladliena Abkadirova. I am a professional artist dedicated to exploring the possibilities of color, form, and texture in contemporary painting.

I currently live in Florida, where the beauty of nature and the ever-changing light inspire my artistic journey every day.

I was born in Russia, where I received my education and discovered my passion for art, design, drawing, and painting. From an early early age, I was captivated by the expressive power of oil painting, a medium that has remained at the heart of my creative work.

Working primarily with oils on canvas, I enjoy exploring different techniques and textures, often incorporating a palette knife to bring depth, movement, and energy to my paintings.

Nature is my greatest source of inspiration. I am especially drawn to landscapes, still lifes, and the beauty of birds and wildlife. Each subject offers an opportunity to capture not only its appearance but also its unique atmosphere and spirit.

For me, painting is much more than creating an image—it is a way of expressing emotions that cannot always be put into words. Every painting reflects a moment in time, an impression, or a feeling that has touched me deeply. It is an invitation to slow down, appreciate the beauty that surrounds us, and connect with the quiet emotions of everyday life.

My work is shaped by memories, light, color, and the subtle impressions that remain in the subconscious. As every artist leaves a part of themselves in their work, each of my paintings carries a piece of my own story.

Painting is also my form of meditation. The moment I begin to paint, the outside world fades away, time loses its meaning, and I become completely immersed in the creative process. It is a source of peace, fulfillment, and endless inspiration.

Through my art, I hope to share these feelings with others and create paintings that bring warmth, serenity, and joy to those who experience them.
