Exhibitions

My First Exhibition

Venice, Number 33 Gallery of Arts • March 2025

I am delighted to share the highlights of my first exhibition at an art gallery. The exhibition featured seven original oil paintings, each reflecting my artistic journey and expressing my unique vision as an artist. This exhibition was a truly special milestone for me and a wonderful opportunity to present my work to a wider audience, meet inspiring people, exchange ideas, and experience the unique creative atmosphere of the gallery.

What meant the most to me was seeing my paintings resonate with visitors, witnessing their genuine emotions, and hearing their personal impressions and stories. Moments like these inspire me to continue creating, exploring new ideas, and sharing my art with the world.

Art Workshops & Lessons

Monthly Art Workshops for Adults

I truly believe that everyone can learn to paint. After all, every child is born an artist—it simply takes encouragement to keep that creative spirit alive. That is why I so enjoy hosting art workshops for adults. During our sessions, I share my passion for painting, introduce participants to great artists and their work, and teach professional techniques in a warm, welcoming, and inspiring atmosphere where everyone feels comfortable exploring their creativity.

My greatest joy is seeing people discover their artistic abilities, gain confidence, and experience the pleasure of creating something with their own hands. By the end of each workshop, every participant leaves with a painting they have created themselves, wonderful memories, and a sense of accomplishment. For many, this first experience becomes the beginning of a lifelong love of painting.

Art Lessons for Children

I offer both private and group art lessons for children of all ages. Teaching children is a special joy for me. There is nothing more rewarding than seeing their eyes light up when they complete a drawing or painting they are proud of. Those happy smiles and moments of accomplishment make every lesson truly worthwhile.

I am grateful to share these wonderful moments of creativity, success, and discovery with my young artists. My goal is to help every child develop confidence, express their imagination, and enjoy the creative process in a warm, encouraging, and supportive environment. Together, we explore new techniques, discover new ideas, and create beautiful artwork. Every lesson is a step toward new knowledge, new skills, and many wonderful paintings.

About me

Hello! My name is Vladilena Abkadirova. I'm a professional artist dedicated to exploring the possibilities of color, form, and texture in contemporary painting.

I currently live in Florida, where the beauty of nature and the ever-changing light inspire my artistic journey every day.

I was born in Russia, where I received my education and discovered my passion for art, design, drawing, and painting. From an early early age, I was captivated by the expressive power of oil painting, a medium that has remained at the heart of my creative work.

Working primarily with oils on canvas, I enjoy exploring different techniques and textures, often incorporating a palette knife to bring depth, movement, and energy to my paintings.

Nature is my greatest source of inspiration. I'm especially drawn to landscapes, still lifes, and the beauty of birds and wildlife. Each subject offers an opportunity to capture not only its appearance but also its unique atmosphere and spirit.

For me, painting is much more than creating an image — it is a way of expressing emotions that can't always be put into words. Every painting reflects a moment in time, an impression, or a feeling that has touched me deeply. It's an invitation to slow down, appreciate the beauty that surrounds us, and connect with the quiet emotions of everyday life.

My work is shaped by memories, light, color, and the subtle impressions that remain in the subconscious. As every artist leaves a part of themselves in their work, each of my paintings carries a piece of my own story.

Painting is also my form of meditation. The moment I begin to paint, the outside world fades away, time loses it's meaning, and I become completely immersed in the creative process. It's a source of peace, fulfillment, and endless inspiration.

Through my art, I hope to share these feelings with others and create paintings that bring warmth, serenity, and joy to those who experience them.
