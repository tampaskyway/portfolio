About me

Hello! My name is Vladliena Abkadirova. I am a professional artist dedicated to exploring the possibilities of color, form, and texture in contemporary painting.

Being an artist for me is a great way of self-expression. By creating paintings, I can share my emotions and feelings that arise at different moments in my life.

These can be various emotions: a range of feelings and a palette of colors that leave an imprint on my subconscious. By reworking these images, I, like every artist, leave a piece of myself on the canvas.

I want to share these emotions with friends, loved ones, and the people around me.

For me, painting is like meditation. When I pick up a brush, I fall into a timeless state; I don't even know how many hours have passed. It brings me great pleasure when I paint.

As for selling my works, there are several reasons that motivate me.

Selling my paintings is recognition of my work. It allows me not only to receive financial reward for my efforts but also to feel that my creativity is important and interesting to other people. It's a way to share my thoughts and feelings with a wider audience.

Moreover, selling my works can provide financial independence and the opportunity to continue creating. It enables me to work in more comfortable conditions, have more resources to create new projects, and develop my skills.
